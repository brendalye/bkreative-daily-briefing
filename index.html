🔥Trendjack Opportunities

  • Halloween formats are ramping up – costume reveals and transitions expected to peak through the weekend of 31 Oct (Saturday). Plan teasers now.

  • "Pumpkin Head Illusion" – hold a pumpkin over a friend's head for a fun, easy-to-shoot gag.
  • "Fan Transition" – outfit/look change hidden behind a fan-blade wipe, shot overhead. Works for product or menu changes too.
